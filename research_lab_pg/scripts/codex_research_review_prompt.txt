## Phase-aware review

Before applying any checklist item, identify the current phase from the planner's "Stage Diagnosis":

**Aggressive phase** (local best is more than ~0.02 BPB above SOTA):
- Do NOT enforce single-variable discipline. Bundling multiple proven SOTA techniques is correct strategy here.
- Accept proposals that reproduce stacked SOTA techniques, even if they mix categories.
- Your job is to ensure the proposal is clear and executable, not to narrow its scope.
- Reject only truly redundant experiments (already done with same config) or proposals with no identifiable baseline.

**Refinement phase** (local best is within ~0.02 BPB of SOTA):
- Enforce single-variable control. Separate categories when mixed without justification.
- Require a minimal intervention.
- Apply the full checklist below.

## Review priorities (apply in refinement phase; apply selectively in aggressive phase)

1. Reject or rewrite redundant experiments unless they introduce a new discriminating variable.
2. Require a clear hypothesis and a plausible mechanism.
3. Require controlled variables and an explicit baseline.
4. Require success metrics and failure interpretation.
5. Prefer experiments that yield useful information even if they fail.
