## Confirmed Signals
- External leaderboard evidence shows that `sliding-window eval` is a large, repeatable gain and is now baseline table stakes for serious runs.
- External leaderboard evidence shows that current top runs win by combining `evaluation + compression-aware export + quantization-friendly optimization + extra capacity`.
- The current root `train_gpt.py` is still close to the simple baseline family: `9L / 512 / MLP 2x`, plain validation chunks, and `int8+zlib` export.
- The current root `train_gpt.py` already reports both pre-export and post-export metrics, so it is suitable for controlled comparison experiments.
- The root `train_gpt.py` now has explicit evaluation modes: `non_overlapping`, `sliding_window`, and `both`, with fixed `EVAL_STRIDE` support and mode/stride/time labels on both pre-export and post-export evaluation logs.
- The external repo under `/newcpfs/lxh/parameter-golf` contains the real FineWeb validation shard at `/newcpfs/lxh/parameter-golf/data/datasets/fineweb10B_sp1024/fineweb_val_000000.bin` and the real tokenizer at `/newcpfs/lxh/parameter-golf/data/tokenizers/fineweb_1024_bpe.model`.
- Local real-asset evidence now confirms that `sliding_window stride=64` is materially better on the same checkpoint and same exported artifact: final pre-export `val_bpb` improved from `1.2515` to `1.2199` (`-0.0316`) and final post-export improved from `1.25689044` to `1.22505202` (`-0.03183842`).

- Local export-only evidence now confirms that changing only the outer container from `zlib-9` to `zstd-22` is lossless and modestly byte-positive on the same real `int8` payload: artifact bytes fell from `15785852` to `15748490` (`-37362`, `-0.2367%`) while post-export `val_bpb` stayed exactly `1.22505202` and quantized tensors matched bit-for-bit after roundtrip.
- Local export-only evidence now confirms that broad `uniform int6` on the locked checkpoint is not quality-safe despite strong byte savings: versus regenerated `uniform int8 + zstd-22`, artifact bytes fell from `15748155` to `12382907` (`-3365248`, `-21.3692%`) but post-export `val_bpb` worsened from `1.22505202` to `1.29818551` (`+0.07313349`), far beyond the allowed `+0.003` band; both artifacts still roundtripped and loaded exactly.

- Local export-only evidence now suggests the selective-export search is near saturation on this checkpoint: adding `blocks.*.attn.proj.weight -> int6` on top of `MLP-only int6` reduced artifact bytes further from `13946844` to `13477497` (`-469347`, `-3.3653%`) and from regenerated `uniform int8` by `-2270838` (`-14.4195%`), but raised post-export `val_bpb` from `1.22768330` to `1.22805392` (`+0.00037062`) and to `+0.00300190` over regenerated `uniform int8`, slightly beyond the explicit guardrail; both artifacts still roundtripped and loaded exactly.
- Local optimization evidence now shows that changing only `WARMDOWN_ITERS` from `1200` to `3000` on the locked baseline recipe modestly improves export robustness under the fixed `MLP-only int6` readout even though the raw checkpoint metric is flat-to-slightly worse: checkpoint `val_bpb` moved from `1.22005399` to `1.22014196` (`+0.00008797`), but regenerated `uniform int8 + zstd-22` improved from `1.22505202` to `1.22427718` and `mlp_int6_else_int8 + zstd-22` improved from `1.22768330` to `1.22696259`; the `MLP-only int6` quantization gap fell from `+0.00762931` to `+0.00682063`.

- The `export_005` retest still improved meaningfully versus historical `export_004` on the old checkpoint: the same `MLP + attn.proj int6` policy improved from `1.22805392` to `1.22740875` (`-0.00064517`) and its quantization gap improved from `+0.00799993` to `+0.00726679` (`-0.00073314`), but the regenerated `uniform int8` baseline improved too, so the explicit guardrail remained missed.

## Negative Or Inconclusive Results
- Pure broad lower-bit export is no longer an open generic question on this checkpoint: `uniform int6` failed badly on quality, so any follow-up lower-bit export must be selective or training-assisted rather than another blind uniform-bit reduction.
- The project no longer needs to ask whether selective export is viable at all on this checkpoint: `MLP-only int6` answered that positively enough to justify one more narrow selective round before pivoting to optimization changes.
- The old conclusion that `attn.proj.weight -> int6` was globally closed for this recipe family is no longer valid: the failure was checkpoint-dependent, and the stronger `arch_002` checkpoint turned the same export rule into a positive result.
- A tiny synthetic smoke run on identical weights and identical exported bytes produced effectively zero `val_bpb` delta between `non_overlapping` and `sliding_window`; this validates implementation and token accounting pathways only, not the real hypothesis.
- An expanded artifact-recovery audit across additional accessible storage under `/newcpfs/lxh` found no reusable real checkpoint/export pair beyond the already-audited repo workspace. The only new experiment-like backup root, `/newcpfs/lxh/physicslm_backups/hardtok_analysis_20260306_040545`, contains analysis outputs and references ephemeral `/tmp/physicslm_hardtok_demo/checkpoints/0000000050` rather than persistent recoverable artifacts.
- A 1-GPU execution of the same real-asset dual-mode baseline was not a practical way to close the question on local L20Z hardware because the full step-0 evaluation was too slow; distributed 8-GPU execution preserved the same scientific comparison while making the run practical.
- The warmdown-only gain is modest rather than transformative: `WARMDOWN_ITERS=3000` improved the fixed export readout by less than `0.001 val_bpb`, so another optimization round should stay single-variable and targeted, or reuse this improved checkpoint only for a narrowly justified export follow-up.
- The Muon-warmup gain is also modest rather than transformative: it improved the primary fixed exported readout by only `-0.00020055 val_bpb` and worsened checkpoint `val_bpb` by `+0.00047447`, so additional micro-schedule tuning on this architecture family is lower priority than a clean architecture-side test.
- `MLP3x` under the current locked export recipe is not submission-viable despite its strong quality gain: regenerated `uniform int8 + zstd-22` grew to `19989838` total bytes and regenerated `mlp_int6_else_int8 + zstd-22` grew to `17326746`, so both fixed readouts miss the 16 MB cap by a large margin.
- `10L / MLP2x` does not make plain regenerated `uniform int8 + zstd-22` byte-safe under the locked protocol: total bytes still rose to `17480480`, so depth alone does not solve the baseline export-cap problem.
- On the successful `BigramHash(4096) + SmearGate` checkpoint, regenerated `mlp_int6_else_int8 + zstd-22` is no longer byte-safe: total bytes rose from `15482717` on `arch_002` to `16084095`, so the stronger architecture consumed enough extra bytes that the old safe readout no longer fits.

## Current Best Known Configuration
- architecture: external frontier clusters around `9L-11L`, width `512`, `MLP 3x`, plus small local feature additions such as `SmearGate` and `BigramHash`.
- optimization: external frontier repeatedly uses stronger Muon momentum warmup, longer warmdown, and nontrivial weight decay to improve quantization robustness.
- evaluation: local baseline should now use `sliding-window eval` with `stride=64` as the fixed standard; the measured local gain on the same real checkpoint/export pair was about `-0.0318 val_bpb` post-export.
- export: the local clean baseline should now use `int8 + zstd-22` instead of `int8 + zlib-9`; the byte gain is only about `0.24%`, so the next likely leverage remains lower-bit or mixed-precision export under the same container.
- export: the strongest local byte-safe export frontier is now `arch_002` with `mlp_int6_plus_attn_proj_int6_else_int8 + zstd-22`, scoring `1.22086139` at `14954974` total bytes; on that checkpoint it improves on regenerated `mlp_int6_else_int8` by saving `527743` artifact bytes for only `+0.00033737 val_bpb`, and it remains within the explicit `+0.003` guardrail versus regenerated `uniform int8`.
- export: the strongest local byte-safe architecture/export combination tested so far is now `arch_003` with `mlp_int6_plus_attn_proj_int6_else_int8 + zstd-22`, scoring `1.21797944` at `15564216` total bytes; it beats the prior locked best by `-0.00288195 val_bpb` but spends `+609242` extra total bytes and shows a larger quantization gap.
- optimization: for the locked baseline recipe, `WARMDOWN_ITERS=3000` is now the stronger local export-robustness setting than the original `1200` if the readout of interest is exported `uniform int8` or `mlp_int6_else_int8`, but the observed gain is modest and does not materially improve the raw checkpoint metric.
- optimization: for the fixed selective-export objective, the strongest local schedule variant tested so far is now `WARMDOWN_ITERS=3000` plus `MUON_MOMENTUM_WARMUP_STEPS=1500`; this slightly improves the fixed `mlp_int6_else_int8` exported score and quantization gap versus `opt_001`, but it does not improve the raw checkpoint metric and leaves regenerated `uniform int8` effectively flat.
- architecture: `MLP3x` is now confirmed as a strong quality lever on the locked schedule/export recipe, but not a byte-safe one; the next architecture-side question should spend capacity more efficiently than widening every MLP block.
- architecture: the strongest local byte-safe architecture/export combination tested so far is now `10L / MLP2x` on top of the locked `opt_002` schedule with `mlp_int6_plus_attn_proj_int6_else_int8 + zstd-22`, scoring `1.22086139` at `14954974` total bytes; this is slightly worse in `val_bpb` than regenerated `mlp_int6_else_int8` on the same checkpoint but materially smaller and still well under the cap.

## Open Hypotheses
- With `BigramHash(4096) + SmearGate` now validated as a real quality gain, the next worthwhile gain may come from a compression-friendly optimization lever such as stronger weight decay or late averaging on this architecture, rather than another nearby feature-path variant.
